## Wizard's Tome

A rare magical item that unlocks the companion movement spells from anywhere.

- Crafted via ritual: sneak-right-click an Enchanting Table in The End.
- Requires a fully powered table (15 bookshelves).
- Hold an Eye of Ender and have in inventory: 1 Book, 1 Diamond, 3 Redstone, 3 Lapis Lazuli. All of them, the Eye included, are used up.
- Once crafted, carry it and press [ near the bot. It unlocks Regroup, Summon (also across dimensions), Home and Remote Inventory.
- Can also be obtained via /give @p frens:wizard_tome in creative.

**Command:** /give @p frens:wizard_tome (creative)

**Where:** Open Spells key [ (works in any world)

## Navigation Artifacts

Give your companion a Compass, Map, or Eye of Ender to unlock autonomous navigation.

- Compass / Map (basic): bot can navigate to its nearest or preferred base, same dimension only.
- Eye of Ender (enhanced): navigate to any named base, cross-dimension, at full fast-travel speed. Either you or the bot can hold it.
- Use /bot open &lt;alias&gt; to give items to your companion via its inventory screen.
- Navigation artifacts are reusable. They are not consumed.

**Command:** `/bot open <alias>`

**Where:** Give item via Bot Inventory screen

## Spell Ingredients

Quick reference for all spell-related items and what they unlock.

- Wizard's Tome (you or the bot carry it): Regroup, Summon (also across dimensions), Home and Remote Inventory.
- Enchanting Table within 4 blocks: the same as the Tome. For Summon across dimensions it must be near you.
- The Tome and table don't replace the pearls, chorus fruit, table or anvil the other spells need.
- Eye of Ender (you or the bot): Regroup, Summon and Home. Not used up. Summon with your own Eye and no Tome or table has a 60s cooldown.
- Goat Horn (you carry it): Regroup by typed command only. The Spells tab greys Regroup out with only a horn.
- Ender Pearl (one each): Remote Guidance, used up.
- Ender Pearl + Chorus Fruit (one each): Chorus Recall, instant teleport, used up.
- Soul of Ender: the bot needs an Eye of Ender and a Chorus Fruit. The fruit is used up.
- Enchant and Anvil need you within 4 blocks of the block.

**Command:** UI-only action (Spells tab)

**Where:** Hold item or be near Enchanting Table to unlock spells
