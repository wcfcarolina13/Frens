# Spells

[All Guide topics](Guide.md)

## Remote Guidance

Uses paired ender pearls to guide your companion across any distance.

- Both you and your companion need at least one Ender Pearl somewhere in your inventories.
- Choose destination: your location, its home, or one of your bases.
- Both pearls are used up when you cast, before the trip is checked.
- It is a fast travel trip with no food needed. A fight cancels it and the pearls are lost. If it is resting from a trip, it leaves when the rest ends. It also needs a way to find the place. Carry a spare pearl each so the pearls still count as that way.
- The trip takes about 1.5 seconds per chunk, more across dimensions.
- Open the Spells tab to cast this spell.

**Command:** UI-only action (Spells tab)

**Where:** Shortcut: [ (key.frens.open_spells)

## Chorus Recall

Consumes paired ender pearls and chorus fruit for an instant teleport.

- Both you and your companion need one Ender Pearl and one Chorus Fruit somewhere in their inventories.
- Choose direction: teleport bot to you, or you to bot.
- Works across dimensions. Always instant, no delay.
- All four items are consumed (one pearl + one chorus from each).
- A bot riding an animal may be refused, with nothing used up.

**Command:** UI-only action (Spells tab)

**Where:** Shortcut: [ (key.frens.open_spells)
