## How the player sleeps

`/tp` to the cell north of the bed foot, then the fork's `look yaw=0 pitch=55` (a `/tp ... facing` does not turn the camera in control mode: the first A attempt looked at the sky and nothing happened), then `keybind key.use`. While the player is in bed the script sends no control-mode input at all (escape on the sleeping chat screen is "Leave Bed"), only `bot_state` and `players` polls. `bot_state` has no sleeping flag: a sleeping body shows y 200.56 on a y 200 bed (standing is y 200.0).

## Other observations

- `Task 'skill:sleep' finished with state ABORTED` after a failed zzz attempt (C and D). The logoff decision still ran (it reads `isAbortRequested`, not the ticket state), so it is only a confusing log word for a normal failure.
- The logoff and setting-off chat lines carry literal `§7 ... §r` codes in `Text.literal`; they show as grey in game and raw in the log, the same as the older zzz lines.
- Bradley's Nirn instance was running at deploy time (23:41) and was no longer running by the first sandbox quit (about 23:55). Nothing here touched it: every quit and check matched only `mcp-sandbox/natives`. Its log was not read.
- The launch guard in `sb_up` refuses while any game runs, so the launches used its body without that guard (the controller accepted running beside Nirn).
